# Pricing notes

## Currency

Convert tenge↔rubles at the current rate and say which rate you used; the rate
moves, so a fixed ruble price needs a margin or should stay pegged to tenge.

## Discounts that are defensible

- 10% when the client takes all stages at once.
- A lower fixed price when the core is already built and tested — that is real
  saved work, not a concession.

## Add-ons seen in practice

- Panel / dashboard with statistics: separate stage, priced apart from the MVP.
- Extra integrations (e.g. Google Sheets mirror) are usually a ТЗ requirement —
  keep them inside the MVP unless the ТЗ is silent about them.
